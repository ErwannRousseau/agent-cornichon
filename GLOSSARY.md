# Agent Cornichon

A team game where colleagues try to slip a secret word into a meeting.

## Glossary

**Operation**

An Agent Cornichon round played during a meeting.

**Organizer**

The person who opens registration and can start the operation.

**Agent**

A player who chooses to join an operation.

**Mission**

An individual challenge to work a secret word into the conversation during an
operation.

**Report**

The agent’s private declaration of their mission result. They succeed if they
say their word, even if someone notices. The first result they submit is final.

**Pickle**

A unit of an agent’s personal score, called a _cornichon_ in Slack.
